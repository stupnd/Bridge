Recognition
- Old: rule-based (bent/straight per finger), recognized only 10 of 26 letters (A B D F I L S U W Y).
- Current: data-driven nearest-neighbour model on calibrated flex readings, recognizes all 24 static letters (not J, Z); rejects matches >25% away; says "unsure" below 15% confidence; 0.3 s smoothing.
